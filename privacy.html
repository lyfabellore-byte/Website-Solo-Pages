Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TEESKOLAR. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website and how we use it. This policy complies with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.

TL;DR: We only collect what we need to process your t-shirt order. We don't sell your data.

2. What Information We Collect

Information you give us: When you use our contact form or order form, we collect your name, email address, contact number, shipping address, t-shirt size, and quantity.

Automatic information: We collect basic data like IP address, browser type, and pages visited through cookies to improve our site.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and confirm your orders and inquiries
  • Communicate with you about your order status
  • Improve our website and products
  • Prevent fraud and ensure site security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to remember your preferences and keep the site running smoothly. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some parts of the site may not work properly.

5. How We Store Your Data

Your data is stored securely and is only accessible to the TEESKOLAR team. We retain your order information for 2 years for accounting and support purposes, after which it is securely deleted.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. If you wish to exercise these rights, contact us at hello@examplewebsite142.com.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but please note that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last Updated” date.

If you have questions about this policy, email us at: hello@examplewebsite142.com
